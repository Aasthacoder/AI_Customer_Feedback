AI-Assisted Customer Feedback → Product Roadmap
Python | NLP | TF-IDF | Sentence Embeddings | Clustering | Product Analytics

- Analyzed 9,995 usable CFPB consumer complaint narratives using Python, NLP and TF-IDF, establishing a text-analysis baseline across 10,000 collected complaints and identifying recurring customer issues and language patterns.
- Generated 384-dimensional sentence embeddings and clustered 9,995 complaints into 10 semantic groups, using similarity analysis and cluster-level validation; the top-5 semantic neighbors shared the same issue 47% of the time.
- Identified purchase disputes/refunds as a 1,222-complaint cluster (12.23%), with 70.95% concentrated in the leading related sub-issue, translating the finding into a Dispute Tracker MVP with success metrics, experiment design, and a phased rollout roadmap.
